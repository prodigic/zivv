# Zivv weekly delivery

Terms for importing show information and publishing weekly additions. This glossary lives in `docs/` alongside the project's delivery documentation.

## Language

**Source edition**: A dated delivery of show information from a publisher, including later corrected deliveries for the same edition.

**Source observation**: Evidence of what a publisher supplied at a particular time, preserved independently of later corrections.

**Import receipt**: The durable account of accepting a particular source batch, including its accepted shows and unresolved outcomes.

**First-added date**: The time a show was first accepted into the catalog. Corrections and repeated deliveries do not replace it.

**Weekly edition**: A frozen selection of shows first added within a particular weekly window. Its membership is independent of subsequent corrections to those shows.

**Publication cutoff**: The end of the latest successfully published weekly window. Preparing or importing an edition does not advance it.

**Release candidate**: A particular version of the site and dataset prepared for validation and publication.

**Verified publication**: A release confirmed to be available and correct at the intended public destination.

**Correction release**: A later verified publication that corrects show information while retaining the original weekly edition's membership and first-added dates.
