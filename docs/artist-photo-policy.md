# Artist photo publication policy

Confirmed by the operator on October 17, 2026, after the decision interview for
[#24](https://github.com/prodigic/zivv/issues/24). This records the agreed policy;
production enforcement and rendering are tracked separately in
[#22](https://github.com/prodigic/zivv/issues/22).

## Eligibility and evidence

Publish a photo only when retained evidence establishes an applicable open
license, explicit permission, or clearly stated promotional-use terms covering
Zivv's intended use. A publicly accessible image, official artist page, press
download, HTTP success or discovery review alone does not establish permission.
Unknown terms remain unknown; they are not an approval or a denial.

Verify the exact artist identity and image context independently of reuse terms.
Do not transfer permission, photographer credit or identity between images merely
because they share a page, artist name or host. Combined bills and namesakes need
an explicit verified identity mapping.

Keep image-specific source URL, owner/photographer credit when established,
license/permission evidence and its date, approved use, required attribution,
transformation restrictions, and review decision. Record any known expiry or
withdrawal. Preserve the exact terms/version relied on in private evidence;
publish appropriate attribution and source/license links, not private correspondence.

## Approval and fallback

Automated checks may approve clear, supported licenses or explicit promotional
permissions only when the recorded terms cover the planned use and all required
conditions can be satisfied. Do not infer permission from availability or from
an AI interpretation without supporting evidence. Ambiguous terms, conflicting
ownership or unsupported restrictions stay queued for operator review.

Keep discovery, identity review and permission approval separate. Research catalogs
are candidate inventories, not publication allowlists. Existing records need
permission review before production use; this decision does not approve the full
catalog or authorize contacting artists.

Artists without an approved usable photo retain the existing fallback. Missing
photos never block a valid show import or Friday publication. Permission outreach
requires separate authorization.

## Delivery, attribution and edits

Copy approved photos into controlled site storage when their terms permit that
delivery. Retain original source and permission evidence. If copying is not
permitted, do not copy; any alternative delivery still needs an explicit approved
basis. Do not silently hotlink an unapproved candidate as a fallback.

Every published photo must expose accessible photographer credit when known,
source and license/permission information. Display required credit alongside the
image. Compact cards may link to fuller attribution, provided the terms permit
that presentation. Never invent a photographer or publish private consent details.

Allow resizing, compression, thumbnails and cropping only as permitted. Preserve
the original privately for approved transformation work. Do not crop out credits
or misrepresent the act. Where cropping permission is unclear, use the full image;
if the required delivery cannot satisfy the terms, keep the fallback. Meet all
applicable attribution, modification-labeling and license conditions for each
approved asset.

## Removal and replacement

Withdrawn permission, an identity mismatch or incompatible terms immediately
disqualify an image in approval state. The next publication restores the fallback;
an urgent removal release is available when necessary. Keep decision history and
supporting evidence privately. Disqualification is not deletion of provenance.

Broken or expired images use the fallback. A replacement needs its own identity,
permission and attribution checks; approval never transfers automatically. Remove
disqualified image references and served copies from the new artifact, and document
any remaining cache/hosting invalidation limitations rather than promising instant
removal from every previously cached client.

## Implementation acceptance

The agreed policy resolves the operator decision in #24. Enforcement remains open
until these requirements are implemented and verified:

- A structured reviewed registry distinguishes unknown, approved, withheld and
  revoked outcomes and binds exact artist/image identities to retained evidence.
- Public exports include only approved assets and permitted public attribution;
  stale identity, missing terms or unsatisfied conditions fail closed to fallback.
- Controlled asset delivery and allowed transformations preserve traceability.
- Artist pages and local-act cards render required credit, reserve dimensions,
  lazy-load and recover from media failures accessibly.
- Tests cover missing/unknown permission, stale identity, attribution, restricted
  transformations, broken images, revocation and replacement.
- A removal/republication rehearsal proves that disqualified content leaves the
  next artifact while canonical show data and Friday importing remain unchanged.

Do not close #24 as fully enforced solely because this document is merged.
