# Venue address research — inventory 1

Checked 2026-10-02. All 217 assigned IDs were researched individually against owner, organizer, government, university and official tourism sources, with actual shared-ledger linked event context checked for identity. 155 verified, 47 partial, 15 unresolved. Null means the research did not establish a safe value; it is not a recommendation to erase historical input.

`verified` requires both an address and official web presence supported by opened sources. `partial` includes indexed primary evidence when direct opening failed, historical address-only results, geographic/room ambiguity, and explicitly cited retained raw input evidence from data/events.txt or data/venues.txt. Raw-only rows do not imply independent owner verification; their websites are null. `unresolved` includes conflicting identities, private undisclosed shows and parser artifacts. No IDs were merged. HTTP blocking is an access limitation, not proof of closure.

Important identity gaps: Amoeba combines San Francisco and Berkeley; California Theater combines San Jose and Santa Rosa; combined Mabuhay/On Broadway spans two rooms; Hopmok lacks an unambiguous branch; Warriors Stadium lacks a proven Chase Center link. These retain null street addresses. The 9 Lives shared ID's linked2026 shows consistently give Oakland and published complete listings use7650 Hawley; that is now partial raw-only address, with old435 23nd Avenue input conflict explicitly noted pending owner confirmation. Napa County Fair raw Napa shows match Napa Town and Country Fair at Expo, rather than Calistoga. Old Greenwood resolves to Sunnyvale's golf-course BBQ using raw event context, rather than Truckee.
