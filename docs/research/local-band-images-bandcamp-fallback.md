# Bandcamp photo fallback research

All 62 assigned records were checked. 149 source-page attempts: 140 loaded and 9 unavailable. 94 remote image candidates visually reviewed in browser contact sheets; no images downloaded. Outcomes: 39 logo-artwork-only; 18 photo-found; 5 no-photo-found.

The pass followed artist websites, social accounts, press pages, and original credited reporting. Image URL presence does not grant reuse rights; all reuse terms are unknown. Signed social-CDN avatar URLs are temporary and small. Leader portraits are explicitly distinguished from ensemble photographs. `logo-artwork-only` and `no-photo-found` apply to the checked sources, not every possible source on the web. Original reporting is treated as a primary photographic source only when subject association and captions establish the performer.

Sleep requires a source correction: the [old Bandcamp page](https://sleepband.bandcamp.com/) now belongs to experimental Melbourne Sleep. The intended metal band is identified by its [Third Man Records artist biography](https://thirdmanrecords.com/collections/sleep), which states formation in San Jose in 1990; the [press agency](https://www.pitchperfectpr.com/sleep) provides a photograph credited to Travis Shinn. Personal Space Man's external portraits were rejected because the [recording credits](https://personalspaceman.bandcamp.com/album/live-at-rickshaw-stop) identify those people as live engineer and album artist.
