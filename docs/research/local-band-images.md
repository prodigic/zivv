## Method and limits

The published public/data local-artist and exclusion lists define this inventory. Three extra display records are present there compared with the raw data lists: Geographer, Skip The Needle (5pm), and Spencer Noble (2pm). No raw artist data, IDs, local classifications or live-site files were changed.

Checked 88 already-recorded Bandcamp source pages, found 85 profile images, loaded all 85 in the browser, and visually separated performer photography from logos, illustrations, scenery, and label/compilation artwork. Profile images are not assumed to be photos, current lineups, or licensed for reuse. Exact name comparison flags aliases for additional review rather than silently matching them. Manual primary-source research supplements the profile scan.

The gallery includes photographic composites and stylized portraits, labeled in source notes. Most image licenses remain unstated. The [Avengers photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Avengers.JPG) is by Robman94 under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/); retain attribution, source and license links, identify changes, and observe share-alike terms for adaptations. It depicts a 2012 performance, not a claim about the current lineup.

[Swingin Utters Bandcamp](https://swinginutters.bandcamp.com/music) supplies the selected group photo; the [Fat Wreck press page](https://fatwreck.com/pages/swingin-utters-press-information) has a download link that returned HTTP 403 and was not selected. [Analog Dog's official Media gallery](https://www.analogdogband.com/) supplies the live photo dated July 2023 in its filename.

Image bytes were not copied into the repository. The gallery references remote sources for review; URL persistence and permissions must be checked when integrating selected assets into production. No publication of photos or external permission requests occurred.
