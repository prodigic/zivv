# Local-act research fields and acceptance

Every catalog act should receive an explicit outcome for images, reviews and
direct artist links. Preserve exact canonical ID and billing name. A missing
review must not hide an otherwise verified listening or social link.

For links, record the observed URL, platform, supporting source URL, checked date,
identity evidence and unresolved platforms. Cover official websites, Bandcamp,
Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, YouTube and available social profiles. Never
construct a handle from a band name. A credited release on a label account can
qualify; the label's whole catalog does not become the band's profile.

Distinguish three questions: does a page concern this artist, does this artist
control the page, and does each outbound destination belong to this exact act?
A publisher article can establish identity without making its footer accounts
artist accounts. An artist site can promote band members, unrelated projects or
vendors. Inspect destination scope before accepting each extracted link.

Solo musicians and their billed ensembles require explicit identity crosswalks.
Reuse only verified billing aliases. Keep evidence and URLs for unresolved cases
in research records, outside the published link registry. A bounded unsuccessful
search is not proof that an artist has no account.

Use inexpensive deterministic collection and cached source context first. Trial
agent models on the same small assignments, recording supported output, failures,
tool calls and rework. Compare token use only when actual telemetry is available.
Use a stronger model to sample at least ten percent of positive records and three
negative records per completed batch, plus all identity flags. If sampling finds
a systematic attribution failure, withhold the affected batch and review every
destination produced by that method before export. Preserve audit decisions and
source context in the research directory.

The reviewed registry is `data/artist-links.json`. The full outcome inventory is
`docs/research/local-act-links-catalog.json`, and links also accompany existing
review entries in `docs/research/local-act-review-catalog.json`. ETL validates
transport, platform hosts, exact ID/name and provenance before producing the
minimal public link fields. Canonical identities and first-added history remain
in the shared ledger.
