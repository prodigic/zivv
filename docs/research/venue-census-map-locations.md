# Manual coordinates for Census unmatched and tied venues

Checked 2026-10-02. Reviewed exactly the 31 No_Match and 5 Tie address groups in .cache/census-review.json, including 48 stable venue IDs from their query aliases. Results: 39 supported location records and 9 null locations. Non_Exact address matches and nonnumeric park research were outside this batch.

Coordinates come from opened official venue HTML GeoCoordinates, coordinate-bearing owner directions links, a National Park Service event page, directly opened OpenStreetMap element JSON, and CC0 Wikidata entity JSON. Google directions destination coordinates were used rather than map viewport centers. Short map URLs were resolved read-only. Map links pointing at remote shuttle parking were excluded.

For OSM ways, the recorded point is the center of the geometry bounding box. Building features are labeled site and campuses, fairgrounds, stadium grounds, parks and general address locations are labeled area. These are neither entrances nor individual indoor stages. OSM node points retain their mapped position. Official street addresses and websites were cross-checked against the existing venue research; aliases remain separate records. Discovery Museum and Discovery Meadow share a published address but use distinct features. The restored Siesta Valley Bowl is explicitly tagged as the former Bruns Memorial Amphitheater.

Nulls document missing primary coordinate evidence or conflicting old locations. Makerfarm's homepage metadata points to an older Mariner Square address; those coordinates were excluded from the Barbers Point venue. Pro Arts' former gallery was not treated as its current centralized venue. No Nominatim searches or raster tile downloads were performed.

Machine-readable output: [census-manual-locations.json](../../data/research/venue-maps/census-manual-locations.json).
