Research used focused act-name searches, a more specific identity or release query when needed, and original review opens. Summaries describe a named recording or reviewed performance; release reviews do not establish how a current show will sound. Namesake results, artist biographies, listings, interviews without sufficient original criticism, and unsupported source snippets were excluded. Review dates and author names remain null when not established from the inspected original. Some sources are individual critics or station reviewers; the Tell Me Tell Me source is a promoter's original review and its provenance is explicit.

Not-found means no defensible review established within the bounded pass, not that no review exists. The four access gaps are Adrian West Band (Divide and Conquer original), Litty DeBungus (Grateful Web), SF Symphony (San Francisco Classical Voice), and Ssleeping Desiress (Dark Entries). Indianna Hale And Dust Collector remains uncertain because the candidate concerns a solo release rather than the assigned ensemble. Short Dogs Grow has a historical Sound Choice PDF candidate that needs exact passage and metadata verification. Prima Materia's opened show article mixes biography and secondary criticism, so it was not used for a musical summary.

An independent checkpoint audit found missed Adrian West and Blunt Force Karma candidates. Adrian's official press link was recorded as blocked when its original remained inaccessible. Blunt Force Karma's original In Spite Magazine HTML, fetched by the auditor, was inspected directly: it explicitly distinguishes the San Francisco band from its Chicago namesake and supports the live summary. The published date was verified from article metadata.

Atomic checkpoints were published after each group of 20 completed acts and at completion. Validation confirms 163 unique assigned IDs in assignment order, null summaries for all gaps, and 25–45 words for every found summary.
