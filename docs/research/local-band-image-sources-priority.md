## Source leads and blockers

### Sick Behavior — 1734115327

- Verified artist page: [Sick Behavior Santa Cruz Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sickbehavior_santacruz/), discovered through the Bay Area Blunt Force Karma account's shared performance reel. Artist name and Santa Cruz handle match the identity being researched.
- Inspected [official post](https://www.instagram.com/sickbehavior_santacruz/p/DeIeJIrxUK8/): concert poster, not a band photo. Profile avatar is a band logo. The visible grid also includes performance reels; a video thumbnail was not promoted to a verified still-photo choice.
- Direct performer-photo URL: not verified. Photographer/reuse terms: unknown. Blocker: inspected still posts were posters; further photo-post selection is needed. Instagram shows a sign-up overlay, although limited public post content remains visible.

### Blunt Force Karma — 766968921

- Correct primary profile: [Bay Area official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/bluntforcekarma.ca/). The [Hotel Utah event page](https://wl.eventim.us/event/lamp-lame-ass-mother-phuckers-the-bracers-blunt-force-karm/698100?afflky=HotelUtahSaloon) explicitly links this handle and names Koda Black, Ryan Krebbs, Chris Reeker and The Mighty Zee.
- [Artist ReverbNation profile](https://legacy.reverbnation.com/bluntforcekarma9) independently identifies San Francisco Bay Area punk, formed in October 2023, and matching members. Its visible profile art is a pictogram of a figure smashing a guitar, not photography.
- Inspected [official Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/bluntforcekarma.ca/p/Dd3DmOTG1Qv/): show poster. Direct performer-photo URL: not verified. Credit/reuse terms: unknown.
- Reject [bluntforcekarma.bandcamp.com](https://bluntforcekarma.bandcamp.com/): that page identifies Chicago, Illinois and belongs to the melodic-death-metal namesake. This is a material identity collision, not a suitable local-band source.

### Jack Killed Jill — 1114772948

- Primary label-hosted artist page: [Bandcamp, Well](https://jackkilledjill.bandcamp.com/album/well), displaying New Red Archives branding and Oakland artist identity.
- [Profile image](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/0010906501_10.jpg) is **In Stereo release artwork containing a small group-photo inset**. It is not a clean standalone band portrait. The main Well cover is staged cover photography, not verified performer photography.
- Standalone band-photo URL: not verified. Photographer/permission for inset or potential cropping: unknown. Request the original group image or locate a current official portrait before selecting.

### Bad Angle — 236813179

- Primary page: [Official Bandcamp, self-titled](https://badangle.bandcamp.com/album/self-titled). Current visible text says formed in Oakland in 2013 and returned from hiatus in May 2026.
- Inspected page has band-logo header and illustrated monster release artwork; no profile-photo image visible. Its Facebook link is an additional source lead, not a verified photo candidate in this report.
- Direct performer-photo URL: not verified. Photographer/reuse terms: unknown. Blocker: primary Bandcamp source has no performer photo.

### Big Tractor — 1442690916

- Identity source: [Folk Punk Kesha compilation track](https://folkpunkkesha.bandcamp.com/track/grow-a-pear). This is a compilation account rather than Big Tractor's own artist profile.
- Visible cover and sidebar images belong to the illustrated Folk Punk Kesha compilation. They must not be mistaken for Big Tractor portrait photography.
- Direct performer-photo URL: not verified. Photographer/reuse terms: unknown. Blocker: inspected source contains compilation artwork and no standalone band-photo source. A Big Tractor-owned page or primary event photographer source is still needed.

### Jaded Justice — 1921911176

- Confirmed primary promoter source: [Up The Creek Records event](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/lions-of-fire-with-darling-im-dying-and-jaded-justice-at-up-the-creek-tickets-1998511372313). Its biography identifies the Bay Area act and members Blake (vocals), Beau (lead guitar), Janice (bass) and PZ (drums).
- Direct band-photo source/URL: not verified. Photographer/reuse terms: unknown. Blocker: indexed promoter content establishes the act but does not establish a distinct attributed Jaded Justice photo; the event image represents a multi-act show. No artist-owned press/photo page was established in these searches.

## Selection implications

The six verified candidates can be reviewed visually before choosing images. Elegant Trash's composite and Lost Puppy Forever's costumed performance should be retained as such in descriptions. Irrevria's album cover is also an actual live photo, but contains title/logo artwork; the separate silhouette profile image avoids treating cover art as a portrait. None of these findings authorize photo publication or establish photo reuse rights. No external contact was made.
