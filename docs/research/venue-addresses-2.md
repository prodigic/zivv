# Venue street addresses and websites: shard 2

Checked 2026-10-02. All 217 assigned IDs were researched: 155 verified, 55 partial, and 7 unresolved. No pending placeholders remain. The machine-readable findings, individual sources, and limitations are in [results-2.json](../../data/research/venue-addresses/results-2.json).

A verified result has a street or official geographic access location and an official website supported by opened operator, city, university, tourism-authority, presenter, or official ticketing sources. Partial rows retain explicitly published raw addresses with `data/events.txt` or `data/venues.txt` citations where independent confirmation was unavailable. An indexed snippet or a failed/empty page alone does not count as an opened address verification. Public social and venue-managed ordering pages count as official web addresses when the operator relationship is documented.
